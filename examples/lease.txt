RESIDENTIAL LEASE - 14 HARROW LANE
(A fictional document, written for the quotecheck example. No real lease is quoted.)

1. Rent
The Tenant shall pay the Landlord a monthly rent of $1,450, due on the first day of each
month. Rent received after the fifth day of the month is late, and the Landlord may add a
late fee of $40.

2. Deposit
The Tenant has paid a deposit of $1,450. The Landlord shall return the deposit within 30 days
after the Tenant has moved out, less the cost of any damage beyond ordinary wear and tear,
and shall give the Tenant a written list of every deduction.

3. Entry
The Landlord may enter the apartment to make repairs, but only between nine in the morning
and six in the evening, and only after giving the Tenant at least 24 hours' written notice.
No notice is needed in an emergency.

4. Pets
The Tenant may keep one cat or one small dog. The Tenant shall pay for any damage the animal
causes to the floors, doors or garden.
