Here is what your lease says about moving out, repairs and guests.

The deposit comes back quickly: "The Landlord shall return the deposit within 30 days after
the Tenant has moved out", and section 2 also promises "a written list of every deduction".

For repairs the landlord may come in

> only after giving the Tenant at least 48 hours' written notice.

Guests are covered too. The lease states that “no guest may stay in the apartment for more than
fourteen nights in any calendar year without the Landlord's consent.”

Cats are fine ("one cat").
